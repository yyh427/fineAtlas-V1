## Scope decisions and retained history

Original source objects, adjudicated roles, dataset labels, mappings and
relations remain separate. Same-name objects with different complete ranges
retain distinct UIDs. Source9 adds no identity equality or label mapping by
virtue of a new hierarchy connection.

Reviewed nominal variants connect through `NATIVE_DESIGN_PARENT`; physical or
evidenced design-purpose classifications use their explicit typed relation.
An official derivation statement supports a navigable nominal parent only when
the complete version/family direction is established. Otherwise its source
reference remains visible as provenance without contributing distance.

Whole-source range matters. A broad military aircraft class defined as
"designed or used" cannot universally inherit a purpose restricted to military
design. Its correct physical aircraft parent is retained. A source's jet aircraft
class is broader than the WordNet jet-plane sense: the reviewed inclusion is
from the narrower WordNet plane class to the broader source class. It does not
assert equality or the reverse inclusion.

The reviewed DHC-8 programme role is a model family encompassing its variants;
variant and configuration identities remain separate. Complete source evidence
also supports specific A340 nominal variant parents. Narrow whole-programme
purpose claims remain separate decisions: a passenger branch or majority of
passenger configurations does not prove a passenger-only complete model range.

The 51 withdrawals identify exact existing assertions. They preserve the raw
source object and assertion content, the original UID, and before/after review
history. They do not declare all original facts false or remove independently
supported alternatives. Proper-name objects with unresolved model/instance
grain remain `UNKNOWN` and under review with explicit evidence. Unsupported
wheel-arrangement-to-locomotive inclusion is not repaired by assigning a physical
vehicle role to an abstract configuration.
